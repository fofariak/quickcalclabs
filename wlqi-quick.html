What Is the Quick Work-Life Quality Index?

The Quick WLQI is a streamlined version of our comprehensive 9-dimension Work-Life Quality Index calculator. It distills the most impactful factors — work hours, deep focus time, meeting load, stress, sleep, boundary control, and timezone alignment — into a 60-second assessment that produces a reliable 0-100 score.

Your score falls into one of five categories: Thriving (90-100), Healthy (75-89), Stable (60-74), Struggling (40-59), or Critical (0-39). For a complete breakdown across all nine dimensions with personalized recommendations, take the full WLQI assessment.
